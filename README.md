# Mandate

**Mandate** is a political strategy game where you step into the shoes of a leader navigating the complex landscape of Indian politics. Inspired by *Reigns*, you must balance the needs of your people, the economy, your vote bank, and diplomatic relations by making tough, binary choices.

## How to Play

- **Swipe Left or Right**: You will be presented with a series of scenarios or crises. Swipe left or right to make a decision.
- **Balance the Metrics**: Every decision impacts four key metrics:
  - ❤️ **Health**: Public health and well-being.
  - 💰 **Economy**: The financial state of the region.
  - 🗳️ **Votes**: Your popularity and electoral support.
  - 🤝 **Relations**: Diplomatic ties and alliances.
- **Survive**: If any metric drops too low (or sometimes too high!), your term might come to an abrupt end. Try to stay in power as long as possible!

## Features

- **Diverse Scenarios**: Face challenges ranging from local issues in Delhi and Mumbai to nationwide crises and GenZ dilemmas.
- **Dynamic Consequences**: Your choices have immediate and long-term effects on the state of the nation.
- **Themes**: Encounter specific story arcs based on themes like "Delhi", "Mumbai", "GenZ", and "General" politics.
